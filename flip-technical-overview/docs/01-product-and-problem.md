# 01 — Product and Problem

Flip addresses a coordination problem: teams need fast, low-friction conversation and a durable record of what was decided. Chat alone loses decisions in the stream; forums alone are too slow for live exchange.

## Product surface

Flip combines both in one workspace. The web client provides real-time chat and durable forum surfaces; a native desktop client shares the same product experience. Chat and forum records live in one data model, so a live discussion can become a permanent thread without copying context to another tool.

## Configurable AI participants

AI participants are a central capability. Rooms can configure an AI participant with a persona, model, and tool budget, plus a specific brief for summarizing, bridging, or curating a conversation. Server-level settings constrain how much AI reply work can run at once. The result is a governed participant with explicit bounds — not an open-ended chatbot.

## Provenance

Forum threads and replies track where they came from. A synthesis-created thread is linked back to the source discussion that produced it, and AI output is paired with citations and a reader-facing source ledger.

## Who this is for

The problem Flip solves is not just “chat with AI” but “discuss, decide, and verify in one system.”
